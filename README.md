### 💡 Areas of Interest & Focus

* **Autonomous Systems** — Resilient edge computing, real-time architectures, and mission-critical autonomy engineered for high-assurance, constrained environments.
* **Embodied AI & Robotics** — Bringing physical intelligence into the real world through physics-first simulation (Isaac Sim), real-time digital twins, and closed-loop control.
* **Space GNC** — Vision models with deterministic safety guarantees for flight navigation, rendezvous, proximity operations, and orbital docking (RPOD).
* **Foundation Models & Tasteful AI Products** — Building where user experience and genuine utility come first: *good product design = best solution > AI solution*.
